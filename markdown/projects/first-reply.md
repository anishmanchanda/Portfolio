# First Reply

[GitHub](https://github.com/anishmanchanda/First-Reply)

Automated freelance-enquiry intake with **Notion** as the control panel.

- A messy enquiry arrives; an LLM extracts budget, timeline and stack, writes a summary, judges fit and drafts a reply.
- A human approves or edits the draft in Notion, and only then does it send over SMTP. Nothing goes out without approval.
- Every event is logged with a timestamp.

**Stack:** Python, Notion API, LLM, SMTP
